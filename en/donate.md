## Ways to give

### 1. Online through PayPal

Donate through the PayPal Giving Fund. Your official receipt will be sent automatically to the email address you provide. The church will not issue a separate receipt, so please keep the emailed receipt.

<a class="button button--dark" href="https://www.paypal.com/ca/fundraiser/charity/3908200" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Donate with PayPal</a>

### 2. Donate by cheque

The church’s English name is **West Toronto Chinese Bible Church**.

Please make your cheque payable to **WTCBC** (our registered trade name) or to the full English name.
