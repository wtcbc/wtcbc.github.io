## About us

We are a group of Christian students, most of whom are studying at or have graduated from York University. We have been blessed to know the Lord Jesus, who has brought love, transformation, and hope into our lives. We are eager to share this wonderful news with you.

We also offer support to new students. Whether you are searching for direction, seeking help in a new environment, looking for friends, or simply wanting a place to rest amid a busy life, we sincerely welcome you.
